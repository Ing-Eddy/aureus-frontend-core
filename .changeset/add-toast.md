Add toasts (`@aureus-core/ui/components/toast`): a `Toaster` mounted once and a `toast` API callable from anywhere, with statuses and promises, click to open, action buttons, custom content and layouts, expandable descriptions and card or tinted appearances. The stylesheet adds the `--success`, `--warning` and `--info` tokens. `Modal` no longer closes when a toast is pressed.
